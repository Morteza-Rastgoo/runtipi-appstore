# Open Generative AI

Uncensored open-source AI image & video studio — a HiggsField alternative

## Overview

Open Generative AI is an uncensored, open-source AI image and video generation studio with 800+ models (Flux, Midjourney, Kling, Sora, Veo). No content filters, no subscriptions, fully self-hosted. A self-hosted alternative to HiggsField.

## Notes

- Uses the community-built image 0xcafebebe/open-generative-ai (the upstream repo builds from source). Generation quality depends on the upstream model APIs you configure in the app.
